FARMER MARKETPLACE

Farmer Marketplace is an online marketplace application that connects farmers
directly with customers.

Farmers can register on the platform and offer agricultural products for sale.

Customers can browse available products and purchase products from registered
farmers.


CUSTOMER REGISTRATION

Customers can create an account using the Create Account page.

To register as a customer:

1. Open the Create Account registration page.
2. Enter the full name.
3. Enter the email address.
4. Enter the mobile number.
5. Select "Consumer" in the "Register As" field.
6. Enter a password.
7. Confirm the password.
8. Click "Create Account".

The password must contain at least 8 characters.

The password and confirm password fields must match.

After successful registration, the user is redirected to the Login page.


FARMER REGISTRATION

Users can register as farmers.

To register as a farmer:

1. Open the Create Account registration page.
2. Enter the full name.
3. Enter the email address.
4. Enter the mobile number.
5. Select "Farmer" in the "Register As" field.
6. Enter a password.
7. Confirm the password.
8. Click "Create Account".

The password must contain at least 8 characters.

The password and confirm password fields must match.

After successful registration, the user is redirected to the Login page.

A newly registered farmer may have an approval status of PENDING.


FARMER APPROVAL

Administrators can review farmer registrations.

A farmer can have one of the following approval statuses:

- PENDING
- APPROVED
- REJECTED

PENDING means the farmer registration is waiting for administrator review.

APPROVED means the administrator has approved the farmer.

REJECTED means the administrator has rejected the farmer registration.


USER ROLES

The application supports three main user roles:

CONSUMER:
Consumers can browse products, view product details, add products to their cart,
manage wishlist items, purchase available products, and view their orders.

FARMER:
Farmers can manage their products and view orders related to their products.

ADMIN:
Administrators can manage categories, users, farmers, products, and orders.
Administrators can also review and manage farmer approval requests.


CUSTOMER FEATURES

Customers can:

- Browse products
- Search products by name
- Browse products by category
- View product details
- View product price
- View product stock availability
- View product brand
- View the farmer associated with a product
- Add products to the shopping cart
- Add products to the wishlist
- Purchase available products
- View their orders


FARMER FEATURES

Farmers can:

- View their products
- Add products
- Edit products
- View farmer orders
- View farmer order details

Farmers must be approved according to the application's farmer approval process
before they are treated as approved farmers.


ADMIN FEATURES

Administrators can:

- Access the admin dashboard
- Manage categories
- Manage users
- Manage farmers
- Review farmer approval
- Manage products
- Manage orders


MARKETPLACE CATEGORIES

The marketplace supports agricultural product categories.

Known marketplace categories include:

- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Dairy
- Spices
- Grains

Customers can browse and filter products according to available categories.


PRODUCT INFORMATION

Products may contain:

- Product ID
- Product name
- Description
- Price
- Stock
- Brand
- Image
- Category
- Farmer
- Average rating
- Review count
- Active status
- Created date
- Updated date

Product availability depends on the stock maintained by the farmer.


SHOPPING CART

Customers can add available products to their shopping cart.

The marketplace information does not define payment methods, shipping policies,
delivery times, or refund policies.


WISHLIST

Customers can maintain a wishlist of products they are interested in.


ORDERS

Customers can view their orders and order details.

Farmers can view orders related to their products.

Administrators can manage and view orders.


MARKETMATE

MarketMate is the AI assistant for Farmer Marketplace.

MarketMate helps customers interact with the marketplace using natural language.

MarketMate can answer general questions about Farmer Marketplace and explain
available marketplace features.
